Laser whitening is the fastest way to brighten natural teeth. Here's what actually happens during a session.

## Before we start

We check your teeth and gums first. Whitening only changes the colour of natural enamel, so existing fillings, crowns or veneers won't change shade. If you have any of those on your front teeth, we'll talk about how to match them.

## In the chair

1. **Protect the gums.** A blue protective barrier is painted along your gumline, and a cheek retractor keeps lips out of the way.
2. **Apply the gel.** A whitening gel goes on the front surfaces of your teeth.
3. **Activate with light.** A dental light speeds up the gel. This is usually repeated in short rounds.
4. **Rinse and compare.** We remove everything and compare your new shade with where you started.

## Sensitivity

Some people feel mild sensitivity for a day or two. It usually settles on its own. Tell us beforehand if your teeth are already sensitive.

## Making it last

For the first 48 hours, go easy on coffee, tea, red wine, dark sauces and smoking. After that, regular cleanings and good brushing keep your teeth bright for longer.
