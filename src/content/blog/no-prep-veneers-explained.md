Most people picture veneers as something that starts with a drill. Traditional porcelain veneers usually do: a thin layer of enamel is shaved off the front of each tooth so the veneer sits flush.

**No-prep veneers skip that step.** They are thin enough to bond straight onto the surface of your teeth, so your natural tooth structure stays as it is.

## Who they suit

No-prep veneers work best when your teeth are already in a good position and you want to change:

- small gaps between front teeth
- uneven or worn edges
- teeth that look a little short or narrow
- colour that whitening alone can't fix

If your teeth are crowded, pushed forward or heavily rotated, a little preparation, braces, or a different treatment may give a better result. That's what the consultation is for.

## Why “conservative” matters

Enamel doesn't grow back. Keeping as much of it as possible means your teeth stay stronger, and it leaves more options open for the future. It's the same reason we favour minimally invasive work across all our treatments.

## What happens at the clinic

1. **Consultation.** We look at your teeth, bite and gums, talk about the look you want, and check whether no-prep is right for you.
2. **Design.** The shape and shade are planned around your face, not a catalogue smile.
3. **Bonding.** The veneers are bonded to your teeth and polished. No anaesthetic is usually needed, because nothing is drilled.

## Looking after them

Brush twice a day, floss, and keep your regular cleanings. Avoid biting very hard things such as ice or bottle caps with your front teeth.
