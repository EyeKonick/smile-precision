Direct composite veneers are shaped by hand right on your teeth. With a little care they keep their shine for years.

## 1. Brush gently, twice a day

Use a soft brush and a non-abrasive toothpaste. Gritty “whitening” pastes can dull the polish.

## 2. Floss every day

Plaque along the edge of a veneer can cause staining and gum irritation. Floss or use interdental brushes daily.

## 3. Rinse after coffee and tea

Composite can pick up stains over time. A quick rinse with water after dark drinks makes a real difference.

## 4. Don't use your teeth as tools

Opening packets, biting nails or chewing pens can chip the edges.

## 5. Wear a night guard if you grind

If you clench or grind at night, a guard protects both your veneers and your natural teeth.

## 6. Come back for polishing

Composite can be re-polished at your regular cleaning to bring back its gloss.
